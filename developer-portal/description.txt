  You Are Not Alone — M. Jackson
  Golden — HUNTR/X
  Love Story - Taylor Swift
  Merry-Go-Round of Life - Joe Hisaishi 

Let me know what other songs you want to add to the list!

This game requires a speaker, so it works only on the Pebble Time 2 and Pebble 2 Duo.
