A simple rhythm game. Just hit the UP or SELECT button to the melody!

Right now you can play the following songs:

  Never Gonna Give You Up — Rick Astley
  You Are Not Alone — M. Jackson
  Golden — HUNTR/X

Let me know what other songs you want to add to the list!

This game requires a speaker, so it works only on the Pebble Time 2 for now. Support for Pebble Round 2 may be added in the future.
